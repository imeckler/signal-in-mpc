REUSING ONE ZERO MASK FOR EVERY CONVERSATION IS UNSAFE

If f_r and f_s receive the same zero polynomial, f_r-f_s never changes across
refreshes. An attacker learns its evaluation at node A in epoch0, B in epoch1,
C in epoch2 (only one corrupt party in each), reconstructs the degree-2
difference, and obtains the secret difference. A known reference record then
reveals another record's secret. CCD uses XOR. Fixed small mask pools leave
analogous public linear kernel relations when the coefficient matrix is fixed.

Experimental constant-setup PRZS candidate (NOT an established mobile theorem):
  refresh/NativePrzs.h, refresh_przs-party.cpp, refresh_przs-checks.cpp,
  refresh/PRZS.txt; TlsMpc/Sharing/MaskReuse.lean.
  Fresh key k_b shared by all except b; per-object domain-separated PRF term
  multiplied by X(X-x_b). At party b the missing term vanishes. At most one
  transition corruption leaves its excluded subset's new key hidden. Cases
  with two transition corruptions require the adjacent-budget analysis.
  Holders privately echo shared setup keys and agree on abort, detecting
  malicious equivocation. Fresh setup/seed erasure required every transition;
  merely changing epoch labels under retained master seeds is broken.
  Setup sends 2400 secret bytes cluster-wide, independent of component count;
  local PRF expansion and writes remain O(number of components). Driver limit
  is 4096 components and a fresh setup PER invocation. A full-inventory
  streaming coordinator is not implemented, so this driver is not yet O(1)
  setup for an arbitrarily large database.
