## Necessary runtime correction

Consequently the garbler can retain wrong masks after a linear chunk, even though the evaluator propagates them. Consuming those outputs in a nonlinear chunk fails c_gamma on honest execution.

`prepare_linear_mask_overlay.py` reconstructs a separate header tree from the exact pinned archive, applies only the recorded namespace compatibility overlays, then moves existing mask propagation outside the positive-AND branch.
